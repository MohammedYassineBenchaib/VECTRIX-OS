# Brand and naming

Status: **VECTRIX is a provisional working name.** It has not been cleared for
use. Nothing in the product, this document or any export claims that the name is
available, registrable or free of conflict.

Research date: 2026-10-26. This is a preliminary desk review, not legal advice
and not a trademark clearance search.

## What was checked

- Public web search for “Vectrix” combined with software, electrical and company
  terms.
- WIPO domain-name decision D2005-0316, which recites US registrations.
- USPTO-facing aggregator records (Trademarkia) for marks owned by “Vectrix Inc.”
- SEC filings that use “Vectrix” as a product name.
- DNS resolution of candidate domains (a resolving domain is evidence of use or
  registration; it is not proof of ownership or availability).
- Competitor landscape for electrical design software.

Not checked: EUIPO eSearch plus (interactive, not queryable here), national
Portuguese INPI registers, common-law use beyond indexed web pages, company
registers beyond what search surfaced, or paid clearance databases. A real
clearance search by an IP professional is required before any launch.

## Findings

### 1. “Vectrix” is already used for software and computer services

- WIPO D2005-0316 records that Vectrix Corporation (Dallas) became Vectrix
  Business Solutions, Inc., and owned US trademark registration no. **2,284,808**
  (registered 1999-10-12), described in the decision as covering **computer
  services**. The decision also notes the registration was still listed as live
  at the USPTO at that time.
  Source: <https://www.wipo.int/amc/en/domains/decisions/html/2005/d2005-0316.html>
- The same decision records Vectrix Corporation (Newport, RI) owning US
  registration no. **2,632,033** (2002-10-08) in **class 35** (franchising
  electric land vehicles) and **class 42** (technical consultations in the same
  field). Class 42 is the services class most relevant to engineering software.
  Source: same page.
- A 1999 Microsoft press release describes “EdgeworX” as “a trademark of Vectrix
  Corp.” in an e-commerce context.
  Source: <https://news.microsoft.com/source/1999/05/13/vectrix-and-microsoft-join-forces-to-enhance-e-commerce-solutions/>
- Trademarkia lists a “Vectrix Inc.” owner with a class 42 application for
  “TRAITZ” (filed 2021, abandoned 2022). This is weak evidence of use but shows
  the name is associated with software services.
  Source: <https://www.trademarkia.com/owners/vectrix-inc>

### 2. “Vectrix” is used for a molecular-design software platform

Protagonist Therapeutics’ 2016 Form S-1 describes a proprietary molecular design
environment called **Vectrix™**, including “a pattern matching algorithm within
Vectrix™”. This is a different industry but the same broad signal: a named
software product.
Source: <https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1377121/000119312516645574/d144462ds1.htm>

### 3. “Vectrix” is a well-known electric-vehicle brand

Wikipedia records Vectrix as an electric vehicle company that ceased US
operations in January 2014. Vectrix Global still presents itself as an EV
motorcycle business. The mark is therefore also associated with electrical
hardware and transport.
Sources: <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vectrix> and
<https://vectrixglobal.com/company/>

### 4. Candidate domains are already registered

DNS lookups on 2026-10-26 returned answers for all of:

| Domain | Resolves? |
|---|---|
| vectrix.com | yes |
| vectrix.io | yes |
| vectrix.app | yes |
| vectrix.pt | yes |
| vectrix.eu | yes |
| vectrixos.com | yes |
| getvectrix.com | no answer |

A resolving domain does not by itself prove the name is unavailable, but the
combination of a registered `.com`, `.io`, `.app`, `.pt`, `.eu` and `vectrixos.com`
means a clean, matching domain set is not available under this name.

### 5. The electrical-design software field is crowded, but not with this name

Representative products include elec calc, E3.cable, AmpSketch, Schneider
Electric’s electrical CAD software, AutoCAD Electrical, EPLAN and ETAP. Search
results: Capterra “Electrical Design Software”
(<https://www.capterra.com/electrical-design-software/>), E3.series
(<https://www.e3seriescenters.com/en-us/modern-electrical-engineering-blog/blog-engenharia-eletrica-moderna/saving-time-and-resources-with-electrical-design-software>),
Schneider Electric
(<https://www.se.com/sa/en/product-subcategory/5110-electrical-design-software/>).
These are context for positioning, not evidence about the VECTRIX mark.

## Assessment

The working name is **not safe to adopt without clearance**. There is prior use
in computer services and class 42, prior use as a named software platform, a
well-known EV brand, and every obvious domain is taken. The risk is not limited
to one class.

Recommendation: keep VECTRIX as an internal codename only. Do not print ™ or ®,
do not present it as a brand launch, and do not register domains or companies
under it. Choose a replacement before any public release.

## Five alternatives (preliminary screening only)

These are candidates for a professional clearance search. None is cleared. Each
was screened with a public web search on 2026-10-26; the result is recorded
honestly, including conflicts found.

| # | Name | Why it fits | Preliminary signal | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Schemetra** | Coined from “schematic”; suggests a drawn electrical scheme without being descriptive | No electrical or design-software company found; only incidental personal and gaming uses | May be considered too close to “schematic”; pronunciation needs testing |
| 2 | **Circuitfold** | Coined compound; evokes a folded plan and a circuit | No company found; only an unrelated token in a component datasheet | Long; may read as a feature rather than a brand |
| 3 | **Ohmora** | Coined from “ohm” + “-ora”; calm, non-generic | OHMORA TECH (small online electronics shop) and an Italian fashion label exist | Same broad electrical space; would need class 9/42 clearance |
| 4 | **LoadLine** | Real engineering and maritime term; serious, technical | ARQ Lab “LoadLine” (AI risk assessment), LOADLINE LLC (retail), a logistics tool and a circuit-breaker frame use the term | Descriptive and used in several fields; weak distinctiveness |
| 5 | **Voltform** | Coined compound; form + voltage; short and pronounceable | A UK company, a fitness coach, a Greek login page, a game and a domain listed for sale | Crowded; domain likely expensive; weakest candidate |

If a decision is needed now, the two worth taking to a clearance search first are
**Schemetra** and **Circuitfold**. The other three are fallbacks with known
encumbrances.

## Clearance checklist before adoption

- [ ] Commission a professional search in the target classes (at least class 9
      software and class 42 SaaS/engineering services) for the EU, UK, US and
      Portugal.
- [ ] Search the Portuguese INPI and EUIPO registers directly.
- [ ] Check company-name registers in the intended operating country.
- [ ] Check app-store names, social handles and the domain set.
- [ ] Check linguistic meaning and pronunciation in PT, EN, FR, ES and AR.
- [ ] Only then add ™ and a brand policy.
- [ ] Record the result and date in this file.

## Renaming cost in this repository

Brand strings are **not centralised**. A rename touches:

- `apps/web/src/components/brand-mark.tsx`
- `apps/web/src/components/projects-view.tsx`
- `apps/web/src/components/workspace/workspace-view.tsx`
- `apps/web/src/app/login/page.tsx`
- `apps/web/src/app/layout.tsx` (metadata and application name)
- `apps/web/src/app/globals.css` (header comment)
- `apps/web/prisma/seed.ts` (organisation name, demo emails, demo password)
- package names `@vectrix/*`, import paths and the lockfile
- demo project codes and seeded data

Recommended before launch: extract the product name and demo identity into a
single `brand.ts` module so a rename is one edit plus a seed change.

## Rules for using the current name

1. Never write “VECTRIX®” or “VECTRIX™”.
2. Never say or imply the name is available, registered or cleared.
3. Keep “VECTRIX” out of legal, contractual or public marketing copy.
4. If the name is shown in the UI, the product must remain clearly a working MVP.
